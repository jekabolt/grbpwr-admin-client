# lekalo — privacy notice

## 1. who we are
lekalo (`lekalo.studio`) runs this service. For privacy questions and rights requests write to im@lekalo.studio.

## 2. our role
- For account data (section 3a) lekalo is the controller.
- For content a team enters into its workspace, the team is the controller and lekalo is the processor. We handle it only to run the service for that team.

## 3. what we keep
a. Account: email, name, password hash, acceptance of terms (version and time), sign-in times and IP addresses in logs.
b. Team data: everything the team uploads, including names and contacts of its own staff, fit models and suppliers. The team is responsible for having the right to enter that data.
c. Service emails: confirmation, invitation, password reset, request approved.
d. Technical logs and error reports.

## 4. why we use it
- To create and secure accounts and confirm emails.
- To review team requests and run each workspace.
- To send service emails (no marketing).
- To provide support, keep the service working, and handle abuse and security.
- To meter and limit AI usage per team.

Legal basis: running the service you signed up for (contract), and our legitimate interest in security and logs.

## 5. separation between teams
Each team has its own address `team-name.lekalo.studio` and its own separate database. One team's data is not visible to another team.

## 6. operator access
The lekalo operator (platform administrators) can open any workspace and see and change its data, for support, maintenance, abuse and security handling. This access is not shown inside the workspace. We do not sell or share workspace content and do not use it to train AI models.

## 7. AI features
Some functions (image and text generation, analysis of sketches and pattern files) send the content the user submits to third-party AI providers through the operator's own accounts. Only content submitted to an AI function is sent. AI usage is metered per team, and the operator may limit or switch off AI for a team.

## 8. sub-processors
| who | role |
|---|---|
| DigitalOcean | hosting, databases, file storage |
| Resend | service email |
| Sentry | error reports |
| AI providers reached through OpenRouter and fal | only content submitted to AI functions |

Data is stored in Frankfurt, Germany (DigitalOcean). Some of the providers above are based in the United States.

## 9. cookies and tracking
No advertising and no analytics trackers in the workspace. Cookies and local storage are used only for sign-in.

## 10. how long we keep data
- Account data: while the account exists.
- Team data: while the workspace exists. On a deletion request we delete it within 30 days; backups expire within 7 days after that.
- Logs and error reports: up to 90 days.
- A declined team request and its address are deleted.

## 11. your rights
Depending on the law that applies to you, you may ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, or object to or restrict its use. Write to im@lekalo.studio. For data a team entered about you, we pass the request to that team, as it is the controller. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Requests are handled by email within 30 days.

## 12. security
Each workspace has a separate database, passwords are stored only as hashes, and operator access is limited to platform administrators. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will tell you as the law requires.
