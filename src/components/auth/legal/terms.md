# lekalo — terms of use

## 1. who we are and what this covers
lekalo (`lekalo.studio`) is called "lekalo", "the operator" or "we" in this text. These terms apply to anyone who creates an account or uses a lekalo workspace. Contact: im@lekalo.studio.

## 2. the service
lekalo is a workspace tool for fashion brands and their production: garment models, tech cards, materials, fittings, production runs, tasks, files and media. Each team gets its own address `team-name.lekalo.studio` and its own separate database. One team's data is not visible to another team.

## 3. accounts and sign-up
- You sign up with an email and a password and confirm the email with a six-digit code or a link.
- Your team request is then reviewed by the operator. The workspace is created only after approval.
- We may decline a request without giving a reason. A declined request and its address are deleted.
- Sign-up requires you to accept these terms and the privacy notice. We store the version you accepted and the time.
- A person joining by invitation accepts the same documents when creating their account.
- Keep your password safe. You are responsible for activity under your account.

## 4. roles in a team
- Each workspace has one owner and members with per-section permissions.
- The owner invites and removes people and can transfer ownership to another member.
- The team decides who has access to what. We act on the owner's instructions in support matters.

## 5. your content
- Content you put into a workspace belongs to your team (the customer).
- You give lekalo only the licence needed to store, process and show that content to your team, and to run the functions you use (including AI functions, see section 7).
- You confirm you have the right to upload what you upload, including names and contacts of your staff, fit models and suppliers.

## 6. operator access
The lekalo operator (platform administrators) can open any workspace and see and change its data. We do this for support, maintenance, abuse and security handling. This access is not shown inside the workspace. We do not sell or share workspace content, and we do not use it to train AI models.

## 7. AI features
- Some functions (image and text generation, analysis of sketches and pattern files) send the content you submit to third-party AI providers through the operator's own accounts.
- AI usage is metered per team. We may limit or switch off AI for a team.
- AI output can be wrong. Your team checks it before any production use.

## 8. acceptable use
You must not:
- upload or share illegal content;
- try to reach other teams' data or get around access controls;
- abuse AI functions by automated or bulk means;
- resell or sublicense access to lekalo.

## 9. price

## 10. pause (read-only mode)
We may put a workspace into read-only mode for abuse, security reasons, or non-payment once paid plans exist. The owner can write to im@lekalo.studio to resolve it.

## 11. leaving and deletion

- You can also ask us at im@lekalo.studio to delete a workspace or an account. We handle such requests within 30 days.
